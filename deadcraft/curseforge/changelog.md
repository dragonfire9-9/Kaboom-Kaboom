**0.1.0 (Beta)**: first public beta.
- 20 hostile creatures, 25 peaceful creatures and 7 bosses, each with a custom rig; every boss has its own walk and attack animation
- 4 dimensions with their own terrain, skies and blocks; 8 biomes, none shared between dimensions; tombs, graveyards, castles, gateways and destroyed cities
- 35 procedurally built landmarks across the Overworld and every dimension, including two boss lairs
- 4 knives and 5 new swords with custom first- and third-person swings
- Guns, magic, melee combos, dodging, enchantments, armour sets and vehicles
- Crypt title screen with live 3D bosses, new HUD, menus and container GUIs
