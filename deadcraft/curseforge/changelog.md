**0.1.0 (Beta)**: first public beta.
- 15 hostile creatures, 20 peaceful creatures and 5 bosses, each with a custom rig and animations
- 4 dimensions with their own terrain, skies and blocks; 8 biomes, none shared between dimensions; tombs, graveyards, castles, gateways and destroyed cities
- 25 procedurally built landmarks across the Overworld and every dimension
- Guns, magic, melee combos, dodging, enchantments, armour sets and vehicles
- New title screen, HUD, menus and container GUIs
