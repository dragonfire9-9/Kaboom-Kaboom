# Dead Craft [BETA]

***There is no morning here.***

**Dead Craft** is a full horror overhaul for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**. Twenty horrors hunt the dark, seven bosses wait in tombs, castles and gateways, and destroyed cities are overrun by the dead. Four new dimensions with their own terrain and skies, eight biomes, guns, magic, vehicles and a dodge-and-combo combat system. On top of that, a blood-soaked title screen, a reworked HUD and menus, a cinematic camera and a full soundscape.

> ⚠️ **This is a BETA.** Expect bugs and balance changes, and **back up your worlds** before installing. Please report issues in the comments.

---

## 🩸 Creatures

**20 hostiles, each with its own behaviour:**
- **The Hollow** only moves when nobody is looking at it.
- **Depart** teleports behind you and brings darkness.
- **Illusion** spawns decoy copies of itself.
- **Ghosts** pass through walls and ignore arrows.
- **The Forgotten** hears twice as far as anything else.
- **The Butcher** charges with a cleaver, **the Gravewyrm** bursts up from beneath you, **the Hanged Man** drags you toward its noose, **the Mire Hag** spits poison and calls parasites, and **the Lamplighter** smothers the light.
- Also Severed, Cracked, Scorched, Parasite, Bandits and Bandit Captains, Wretched, Blood Demons, Star Fall and Collapse.

**Rotwalkers** fill the destroyed cities, and they don't burn in daylight.

**25 peaceful creatures** to find, breed and hunt. Ashgrazer, Gloomhog, Cinderhen, Marrow Ox and Duskhare drop meat you can cook.

Every creature has a hand-built, jointed rig with keyframed walk, idle, attack and spell animations, and secondary motion: swaying tails, orbiting shards, flickering flames and halos.

## 💀 Bosses

Seven bosses, each with **its own model and its own walk and attack animation**, a boss bar, a second phase at half health and its own trophy:
- **Fracture:** a crystal titan with blade arms, hovering over its shards. Shard novas and ground slams; summons Cracked.
- **Chronos:** a slowing aura, homing time bolts and teleports. Rewinds its own health once.
- **The Skeleton:** the Ossuary Colossus, a hunched giant of bone with a horned, iron-crowned skull, a ribcage caging soul fire, shackles trailing chains and a jawbone cleaver. It fires bone volleys, raises skeleton guards and leaps at you.
- **Dim Light Demon:** a winged, goat-skulled demon lord. Darkness pulses, hellfire rain and Blood Demon summons.
- **Petrified:** a serpent-bodied gorgon statue with spear and shield. Looking at it slows you to a crawl; it throws boulders.
- **Hive Mother:** a crawler queen in the Hive Nest. Acid, parasite swarms, slams and leaps.
- **Wailing Bride:** a spirit in a rotted wedding gown in the Bride's Chapel. Her wail hurls you back and blinds you.

Bosses spawn in structures, or you can summon them with craftable **Sigils**.

## 👂 They can hear you

Creatures react to sound. Sprinting, gunshots, engines and spells all carry. Text chat carries 16 blocks, and sneaking halves every noise you make. With **Simple Voice Chat** installed (optional), creatures hear your real voice: the louder you talk, the further it carries.

## 🌑 World

Four dimensions, each with **its own terrain generator, blocks, sky and biomes**, so none of them is a reskinned Nether or End:
- **Hell's Paradise:** a gutted cavern world of charnel stone, with roof pillars, stalactites, blood falls and a **sea of blood**. Its biomes are Scorched Forest and Bleeding Hollows, where ribcages of giant beasts jut from sinew floors.
- **Skyfall Torn:** pale islands adrift beneath a shattered moon, with ghostwood trees and stalactites hanging underneath. Its biomes are Floating Islands and Shroud Reach.
- **Light's Descent:** terraced mesas and sheer cliffs at eternal dusk, under an enormous blood moon. Its biomes are Dark Plains and Deserted Cliffs.
- **Disposed Skies:** shattered plates and needle spires adrift in the void, watched by a turning void eye. Its biomes are Storm Field and Glass Wastes.

There are 20 new terrain blocks and plants, and blood is a real fluid you can carry in a bucket.

**Structures:** tombs with buried crypts, graveyards, ruined castles, gateways, portal shrines and **Destroyed Cities**. The cities come in five styles (burned, collapsed, blighted, butchered and meteor-struck) and are full of zombies, loot and wrecked cars.

**35 landmarks**, generated procedurally so no two are alike, each with its own loot and guardians:
- **Overworld:** Plague Chapel, Hanging Tree, Bandit Camp, Hag's Hovel, Sunken Crypt, Bloodied Farm, Bone Pit, Ritual Circle, Ruined Watchtower, Fallen Star, Slaughterhouse, Quarantine Camp, Scarecrow Field and Lamplighter's Road.
- **Hell's Paradise:** Flesh Shrine, Impaler Field, Demon Forge, Cage Gallows and the Hive Nest.
- **Skyfall Torn:** Sky Temple, Bell Tower, Wrecked Airship, Observatory, the Bride's Chapel and the Skyray Roost.
- **Light's Descent:** Cathedral Ruin, Mesa Outpost, Gallows Square, Abandoned Mine, Wyrm Burrow and Hangman's Hill.
- **Disposed Skies:** Void Obelisk, Storm Spire, Shattered Sanctum and the Crystal Garden.

**4 ores:** Necrite, Bloodstone, Voidsteel and Stardust, each with an armour set and a full-set bonus.

## ⚔️ Combat, guns and magic

- **Dodge** with invulnerability frames (Left Alt).
- **Combo chains:** every quick hit adds damage, and the fifth is a finisher that depends on the weapon (cleave, lifesteal, void, starfall, reap, time or shatter).
- **16 melee weapons** with 3D models in hand, including **4 knives** (backstab for double damage, every cut bleeds) and **5 new swords** (sweeping cuts, stacking bleed, chain lightning).
- **Realistic weapon models:** bevelled, tapering blades with fullers, shaped guards, wrapped grips and shaded steel; the guns are rebuilt with octagonal barrels, a fluted cylinder, a scope and more.
- **New view models:** your own arms hold every weapon; guns in both hands, and sneaking aims down the sights.
- **Performance:** animation, glow, item and sky rendering optimised; lighter ambient particles.
- **Player animations:** a lifelike walk, a leaning sprint and idle breathing in third person, and swings for everything in hand (knives stab, fists jab, axes and heavy blades chop overhead, swords cut wide arcs) in both first and third person.
- **4 guns** (revolver, shotgun, rifle, SMG) with view models, real ammo, reloads and recoil.
- **Magic:** read the Grimoire of Awakening to unlock mana, then use six spells: homing souls, fireballs, frost novas, blinks, lightning and healing.
- **7 enchantments**, including Bloodthirst, Soul Reaper, Exorcism and Hollow Point.
- **Vehicles:** the Ruin Runner car and the Sky Skiff.

## 🎬 Presentation

- **Title screen:** a candle-lit crypt where a live, animated 3D model of each boss takes its turn on the altar.
- **HUD:** health is a sloshing vial of blood, hunger a vial of bile, and a skull medallion between them burns as you near death. Armour shows as shields and mana as a vial of souls.
- **GUIs:** every container is repainted in bone and blood, every menu sits over a blood-moon panorama, and all buttons and widgets are re-skinned.
- **Camera:** heavy cinematic walk and run sway, landing dips, dodge rolls and screen shake.
- **Effects:** custom particles, glowing eyes and 58 custom sound events.

## 📦 Requirements

- **Minecraft 1.21.1**
- **NeoForge 21.1.x**
- Optional: **Simple Voice Chat** for proximity voice

Install it on both the client and the server. Minecraft shows an "experimental settings" warning the first time you open a world, because of the custom dimensions. This is normal.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Dodge | Left Alt |
| Reload | R |
| Fire gun / cast spell | Right-click |
| Drive vehicle | Right-click to mount, WASD |
| Pick up vehicle | Sneak + punch |

All visual effects (camera sway, film grain, HUD, title screen) can be adjusted in `config/deadcraft-client.toml`.
