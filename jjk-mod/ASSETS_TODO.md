# Assets to replace

Everything below works now as a procedural placeholder. Replace files in place (same path and name) and
the mod picks them up. If you remake a GeckoLib model in Blockbench, keep the bone names listed, or
update the animations to match.

## Models (GeckoLib, Blockbench "GeckoLib Animated Entity" project)

`assets/jjk/geo/entity/<id>.geo.json` + `animations/entity/<id>.animation.json` + `textures/entity/<id>.png`

| Model | Required animations | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fly_head, roppongi_curse, finger_bearer, rainbow_dragon, smallpox_deity, transfigured_human | idle, walk, attack | curses |
| divine_dog, nue, toad, max_elephant, shadow_rabbit | idle, walk, attack | shikigami |
| sukuna, mahito, jogo, hanami, kenjaku, toji, mahoraga | idle, walk, attack, cast, roar | bosses (Mahoraga's "cast" turns the `wheel` bone, plus a looping `spin`) |

Outfits (Blockbench "GeckoLib Armor" template; bones `armorHead`, `armorBody`, `armorRightArm`,
`armorLeftArm`, `armorRightLeg`, `armorLeftLeg`, `armorRightBoot`, `armorLeftBoot`):
`geo/item/armor/<set>.geo.json` + `textures/item/armor/<set>.png` for jujutsu_uniform, gojo_outfit,
sukuna_kimono, toji_outfit, nanami_suit, mahito_patchwork, geto_kesa.

## Textures

  cursed_fragment, ce_shard, ce_bottle, talisman, prison_realm, death_painting, special_grade_object,

  (16x16, one per technique, 18 total)
- **NPC skins (64x64 player layout)** `textures/entity/npc/`: gojo, yuji, megumi, nobara, nanami, maki,
  panda, inumaki, yaga, curse_user
- **Overlay** `textures/entity/sukuna_markings.png` (64x64 player skin overlay)
- **Particles** `textures/particle/`: cursed_energy_0-3, glow_0-3, spark_0-3, slash_0-3, ring_0,
  lightning_0-2 (glow, spark, slash, ring and lightning are drawn white and tinted in code)
- **Blocks** `textures/block/domain_barrier.png`, `domain_floor.png`
- `textures/models/armor/curse_glasses_layer_1.png` (vanilla armor layer for the glasses)
- `jjk_logo.png` (mod list logo)

## Sounds (`assets/jjk/sounds/`, mono .ogg)

- `roll/` open, tick, reveal, reveal_rare
- `ability/` cast, impact, dash, clap, heal; `ce/depleted`, `grade/up`, `black_flash`
- `technique/<ability>.ogg`: one per ability (50), e.g. blue, red, hollow_purple, dismantle, cleave,
  fuga, divine_dogs, dont_move, piercing_blood, swap, ...
- `domain/` expansion, shatter, and one per domain: unlimited_void, malevolent_shrine,
  chimera_shadow_garden, self_embodiment_of_perfection, coffin_of_the_iron_mountain, incomplete_domain
- `curse/<curse>/` ambient, hurt, death for each of the 6 curses
- `item/` finger_eat, sukuna_laugh, prison_realm, talisman_burn
- `boss/` roar, mahoraga_adapt
- `music/boss/<boss>.ogg`: a 24 s loop per boss (sukuna, mahito, jogo, hanami, kenjaku, mahoraga, toji),
  streamed. Replace with real tracks and adjust `MUSIC_INTERVAL` in `BossEntity` to the new length.

## Structures (`data/jjk/structure/*.nbt`)

Generated by `tools/gen_structures.py`. To hand-build instead: save a structure with a Structure Block and
replace the `.nbt` file. Keep the chest loot tables (`jjk:chests/...`) and the mob spawners and entities if
you want the same gameplay. Files: tokyo_jujutsu_high, kyoto_jujutsu_high,
shibuya_station_{jogo,mahito,kenjaku}, abandoned_school, abandoned_hospital, cursed_tunnel, sukuna_shrine,
curse_womb_detention_center, culling_game_colony.
